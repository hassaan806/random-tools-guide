# Free Online Random Tools (No Signup Required)
A curated collection of simple and fast random tools for decision making, giveaways, classrooms, and everyday use. No downloads, no signups — just open a tool and use it instantly in your browser.

## 🔢 Random Number Generator
Generate random numbers instantly for games, giveaways, raffles, or classroom activities. Set a custom min/max range, generate single or multiple numbers, and get results with one click — no signup or download needed.

👉 Try the [Random Number Generator](https://toolcenterhub.com/random/number-generator)

## 🎡 Wheel Spinner
Spin a wheel to randomly pick names, options, or decisions in seconds. Add your own list of entries, customize the wheel, and use it for giveaways, classroom picks, or settling group decisions fairly.

👉 Try the [Wheel Spinner tool](https://toolcenterhub.com/random/wheel-spinner)

## ✅ Yes or No Generator
