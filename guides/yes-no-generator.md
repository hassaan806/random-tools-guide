# Yes or No Generator Guide

A yes or no generator is a simple tool used to make quick decisions without overthinking.

## When to Use It

* Making small daily decisions
* Choosing between two options
* Breaking indecision quickly
* Fun games and challenges

## Why Use a Yes/No Tool?

* Removes bias from decision making
* Saves time
* Adds randomness and fun

Instead of overanalyzing, you can rely on a simple yes/no result instantly.
